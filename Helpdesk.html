Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding statutory deadlines, document deficiencies, and inspection SOPs.

What happens if my industrial clearance is delayed beyond the statutory 14-day window?
Under the National Single Window Citizen Charter, applications not disposed of within the statutory SLA (typically 14 to 21 working days depending on sector) trigger an automatic Tier-2 escalation to the State Nodal Officer. If no valid statutory query or deficiency notice is raised by the department, the provision for Deemed Approval may be invoked through this portal.
How do I submit revised documents after receiving a Deficiency Notice?
Log in to your Applicant Portal using your registered credentials. Navigate to Clearances > Action Required. Click the specific clearance card displaying the "Deficiency Raised" status, review the reviewing officer's statutory annotations, and upload the rectified PDF document. A fresh submission receipt will be generated automatically.
Are digital signatures (Class-3 DSC) mandatory for all statutory clearances?
Yes. Under the Information Technology Act 2000, all central and state industrial license applications require authorized sign-offs. Applicants must authenticate either via a registered Class-3 USB DSC Token or through the Aadhaar-based e-Pramaan e-Sign service embedded directly within the portal.
Whom should I contact if an on-site joint physical inspection is required?
IndustryOne conducts synchronized joint physical inspections across Pollution, Fire, and Factory directorates. Once your preliminary scrutiny is complete, you will receive an automated SMS/email with the inspection itinerary at least 48 hours prior. You can also contact your District Nodal Facilitation Officer listed in your dashboard.
How can Verification Officers and Field Inspectors obtain administrative portal access?
Regulatory department officers must register through the Administrative Registration Desk using their official .gov.in / .nic.in email address and Departmental Employee ID. Once verified by the State Administrator, access credentials to the Officer Console will be provisioned.
